Fertility Risk Assessment

A quick, educational tool to help you understand potential fertility risk before cancer treatment. This is not medical advice.

--

This tool provides educational guidance only and is not a medical diagnosis. For personalized advice, speak with your oncologist or a fertility specialist.

Next steps & resources

  • Contact a fertility preservation clinic for an urgent consultation.
  • Ask your oncologist how treatment might affect ovarian reserve.
  • Learn about egg freezing (oocyte cryopreservation) and other options.
