Egg freezing is a medical procedure where eggs are stimulated, retrieved from the ovaries, and frozen for future use. The process usually takes 10–14 days before cancer treatment begins.

In most cases, egg freezing takes about 2–3 weeks. Doctors coordinate with oncologists to ensure minimal delay. It is important to discuss timing with your healthcare team immediately after diagnosis.
